Lone Lone Passenger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Lone Lone Passenger ("the App") is a passenger application for requesting transportation services. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how that information is used, stored, and deleted.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The App may collect and store the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Account authentication information
  • Profile photo, if you choose to provide one
  • Current or requested pickup location
  • Destination location
  • Pickup and destination addresses
  • Ride request and trip information, including fare estimates, completed trip fares, and selected payment method where applicable
  • Saved places you create
  • Ratings and comments you submit
  • In-app chat messages you send to or receive from drivers during a trip
  • Notifications related to your account and trips
  • Push notification registration tokens and related app or device identifiers used for message delivery
  • Information necessary to process and maintain your account

3. Location Information

The App may use your device's location information to provide transportation-related features, including determining your pickup location, displaying your location on a map, and supporting trip functionality.

Location access is used only when required for the relevant App functionality and according to the permissions granted on your device. During an active ride request or trip, the App may use a visible Android foreground service notification to support location-dependent trip features if the App is minimized. The App does not request Android's always-on background location permission.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by the App may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your passenger account
  • Process transportation requests and maintain related fare and trip records
  • Connect passenger trip requests with drivers
  • Provide pickup and destination information
  • Display maps and route information
  • Send trip and account notifications
  • Maintain saved places
  • Process ratings and feedback
  • Deliver trip-related messages exchanged between passengers and drivers
  • Register devices for and deliver trip or account notifications
  • Maintain trip records
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security and operation of the App

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

The App uses Appwrite services for account authentication, database storage, file storage, and application-related backend services.

The App also uses Google Maps services to provide map, location, and route-related functionality.

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a Google service, to deliver notifications about trip and account events. Push registration tokens and associated app or device identifiers may be processed for notification delivery and service operation.

The App does not currently display in-app advertisements and does not use Google AdMob.

Information may therefore be processed by these services as necessary to provide the functionality of the App.

6. Fare and QR Payments

The App can show a driver's KBZPay QR code for a passenger to make a payment directly to the driver. Lone Lone does not process that KBZPay payment within the App, collect the passenger's bank account or payment card details for it, or receive a payment-success confirmation from KBZPay through the App.

The App may still store trip fares and the selected payment method as part of the ride request or trip record. Payments made through KBZPay are subject to the payment provider's own privacy practices.

7. Profile Photos and Uploaded Files

If you choose to add a profile photo, the photo is stored as part of your passenger account and is used to provide profile-related functionality.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the account deletion process described below.

8. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared within the Lone Lone service when necessary to provide transportation services. For example, information required to process a ride request may be made available to the driver handling that request. Trip pickup and destination details, trip status, and messages sent through the in-app trip chat may be visible to the driver assigned to the trip as part of providing the requested service.

Information may also be processed by technical service providers used to operate the App, including Appwrite, Google Maps, and Firebase Cloud Messaging services, as necessary for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and application data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, maintain account functionality, operate trip-related features, and meet legitimate operational requirements.

When an account is deleted, personal account information and directly associated personal records are processed for deletion. Certain historical trip or operational records may be retained in an anonymized form where necessary for service records and operational purposes.

11. Account Deletion

You can delete your Lone Lone Passenger account from within the App.

Account deletion removes the passenger account and associated personal data according to the application's account deletion process. Certain historical records may be anonymized rather than completely removed where necessary to preserve trip or operational records.

You may also submit an account deletion request through our external account deletion page:

Lone Lone Passenger Account Deletion

12. Age Eligibility and Children's Privacy

Lone Lone Passenger is intended for people aged 18 years and older. People under 18 should not register an account or use the App.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has registered or provided personal information, please contact us at loneloneoway@gmail.com so that we can review the report and take appropriate action, including account and personal data deletion where applicable.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the App, its services, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with the applicable updated date.
