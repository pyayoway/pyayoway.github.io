PYAY OWAY DRIVER Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

PYAY OWAY DRIVER ("the App") is the driver application used by drivers who provide transportation services through Pyay Oway. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, retains and deletes personal and device-related information.

2. Information We Collect

The App may access, collect or store the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number and account authentication information
  • Driver profile photo
  • Vehicle number
  • Address entered in the driver profile
  • KBZPay QR image uploaded by the driver
  • Current and recent device location used for driver availability and trip operation
  • Ride requests, accepted trips and trip status information
  • Trip history and trip-related operational records
  • Driver wallet and transaction-related records used by the service
  • Driver chat and trip chat messages
  • Ratings and comments associated with driver activity
  • Notification and push-messaging identifiers needed to deliver app notifications
  • Information necessary to maintain, secure and operate the driver account

3. Location Information and Background Location

Location is a core feature of PYAY OWAY DRIVER. When a driver is online or handling a trip, the App uses device location to show the driver's current position, connect the driver with nearby ride requests, support pickup and destination navigation, update trip progress and provide live-location information required for the transportation service.

On supported devices and when the required permission has been granted, the App may continue to access location while the App is in the background or while the screen is off. This background location access is used for active driver availability and trip-related functionality and is not used for advertising.

Drivers can control location permission through Android system settings. Disabling required location permission may prevent online driver and trip-related features from working correctly.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate and maintain a driver account
  • Display and maintain the driver's profile
  • Show driver availability and location to support ride matching
  • Provide pickup, destination, route and trip functionality
  • Process ride acceptance and trip status changes
  • Maintain trip, wallet and transaction records
  • Support driver-to-passenger and driver group communication
  • Display ratings and service-related feedback
  • Send ride, trip, chat, account and service notifications
  • Provide customer support and investigate operational issues
  • Maintain the security, reliability and operation of the App

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

The App uses Appwrite services for account authentication, database storage, file storage, realtime application data and application-related backend services.

The App uses Google Maps services for map, route and location-related functionality. The App also uses Firebase Cloud Messaging or related Google messaging services to support push notifications.

Information may be processed by these service providers only as needed to provide the functions described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Profile Photos and Uploaded Files

Profile photos and KBZPay QR images uploaded by a driver are stored as part of the driver's account and are used for driver profile and payment-related functionality within the Pyay Oway service.

7. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared within the Pyay Oway service when necessary to provide transportation services. For example, information needed for an accepted trip may be made available to the passenger associated with that trip.

Information may also be processed by technical service providers used to operate the App, including Appwrite, Google Maps and push-messaging services, as necessary for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

Pyay Oway does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

8. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal, account and application data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide driver account and transportation services, maintain trip-related and operational functionality, support security and meet legitimate operational requirements.

Drivers may request deletion of their account and associated personal data. When an account is deleted, personal account information and directly associated personal records are processed for deletion. Certain historical trip or operational records may be retained in an anonymized form where necessary for service records, security or operational purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

PYAY OWAY DRIVER is intended for eligible drivers and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for use as drivers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the App, its services or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with the applicable updated date.
