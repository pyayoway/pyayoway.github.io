PYAY OWAY DRIVER Account Deletion

PYAY OWAY DRIVER users can delete their account directly from the Driver App. This page also provides an external way to request account deletion without reinstalling or opening the App.

Delete from the Driver App

Open PYAY OWAY DRIVER, sign in to your account, open Driver Profile, and choose Delete Account. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

An active trip must be completed or ended before account deletion can be processed.

Request Deletion Without the App

Send an email to our support address from an address you can access and include the registered Driver phone number.

  • Your name
  • Your registered Driver phone number
  • The subject: Driver Account Deletion Request

Request Driver Account Deletion

Email: pyayoway@gmail.com

Data Deleted With the Driver Account

Account deletion may include:

  • Driver authentication account
  • Driver profile information, including name, phone number, vehicle number and address
  • Driver public online/location profile
  • Driver profile photo
  • KBZPay QR image uploaded by the driver
  • Driver-created group chat and trip chat messages where deletion is applicable
  • Other personal data directly associated with the active Driver profile

Information That May Be Retained

Completed trip history, wallet and transaction records, commission records, ratings, and other historical operational records may be retained where necessary for legitimate operational, security, accounting, legal, or regulatory purposes. Retained records are not used to recreate an active Driver account.

More information is available in the PYAY OWAY DRIVER Privacy Policy.
