Pyay Oway Passenger Account Deletion

This page provides an external way for Pyay Oway Passenger users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to our support address and include the registered phone number for the Pyay Oway Passenger account you want to delete.

Please include:

  • Your name
  • Your registered phone number
  • The subject: Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

Email: pyayoway@gmail.com

What Happens After Your Request

We will review the request and verify that it relates to the Pyay Oway Passenger account identified in the request.

Once the request is verified, the account and associated personal data will be processed for deletion according to our account deletion procedures.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the information associated with the account, deletion may include:

  • Passenger account information
  • Name and phone number associated with the account
  • Profile photo
  • Saved places
  • Passenger notifications
  • Personal information associated with ride requests
  • Other personal information associated with the passenger account

Information That May Be Retained

Certain historical or operational records may need to be retained where necessary for legitimate operational, security, legal, or regulatory purposes. Where appropriate, personal information in retained historical records will be anonymized.

More information about data collection, use, retention, and deletion is available in our Privacy Policy.
